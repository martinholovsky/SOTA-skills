## Settled during editing and review (2026-10-25)

Measured by the editing passes and re-run by an independent reviewer:
- **SwiftLint** `superfluous_disable_command` and `blanket_disable_command` are on by default, at warning severity (rule docs). This was UNVERIFIABLE above; it is now VERIFIED. `blanket_disable_command` accepts a disable that is scoped (`:next`/`:this`/`:previous`) OR closed by a matching `enable`.
- **Ruby** json 2.18.0 / Ruby 4.0.6: the first deep `JSON.parse!` raises a rescuable `SystemStackError`; a second one in the same process killed it (exit 132, 3 of 3).
- **.NET** SDK 10.0.402, `latest-Recommended` + `TreatWarningsAsErrors`: an xUnit test name with underscores fails on `error CA1707`.
- **Go**: a panic in a child goroutine prints `panic:`, not `fatal error:`. It kills the process past middleware, but it is an unrecovered panic, not a runtime fatal error.

The reviewer also confirmed seven defects in the first draft of the edits, all fixed before merge: four probes that missed their bad case or flagged their good case (.NET `MaxDepth`, js/ts `npm install` inside `pnpm install`, js/ts input-sized allocation, PHP `json_decode` depth); two contradictions with existing text (PHP `--prefer-lowest`, the SwiftLint blanket rule); and the Go "fatal error" wording. The fixed probes were re-run under BSD grep: every bad case hit, no good case did.
